# CyberScope 🔐

### Evidence-Driven Cyber Threat Research & Analysis Platform

CyberScope is a Python-based cybersecurity research platform designed to help users explore documented cyber incidents, understand reported attack techniques, and examine the evidence behind individual findings.

Cybersecurity information is often scattered across incident reports, security advisories, and research publications, making it difficult to connect specific claims to their original sources or compare how different cyberattacks were carried out. CyberScope aims to make this research process more structured, transparent, and accessible.

### What Does CyberScope Do?

* **Incident Research:** Organises information about documented cyber incidents, including their categories, summaries, findings, and reported attack techniques.
* **Evidence Traceability:** Connects individual findings to their associated sources so users can examine where information comes from.
* **Incident Search:** Allows users to search incident records using relevant keywords.
* **Technique Comparison:** Aims to help users compare documented techniques across incidents and identify similarities in reported attack behaviour.
* **Evidence-Aware Analysis:** Records evidence status separately from claims, helping distinguish supported information from disputed, unverified, or insufficiently documented findings.

### Project Goal

The goal of CyberScope is to develop a structured, evidence-focused approach to cyber threat research that prioritises source transparency and careful interpretation of security information.

The project is being developed incrementally using Python, with an emphasis on reliable data handling, testing, and meaningful functionality.

**Project Status:** Under active development. Features are being implemented and tested progressively.
